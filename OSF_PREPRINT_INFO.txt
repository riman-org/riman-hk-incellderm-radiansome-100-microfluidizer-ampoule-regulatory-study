- Title:
RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical Efficacy, Microclimate Adaptation, and Regulatory Trust Architecture of the INCELLDERM Radiansome 100 Microfluidizer Ampoule in the Hong Kong Market

- Abstract (内嵌 GitHub 仓库入口、交互式报告及双基准 DOI):
Background: The adoption of advanced delivery systems like microfluidization in humid subtropical markets requires rigorous validation of both sensory performance and regulatory transparency. This study evaluates consumer perception of the RIMAN Skincare brand's flagship INCELLDERM product, the Radiansome 100 Microfluidizer Ampoule, specifically focusing on its Centella Asiatica (Giant BYoungPool) complex and Niacinamide formulation in Hong Kong. Methods: A stratified survey of 200 participants across diverse skin types, income tiers, and MBTI profiles was conducted to assess texture comfort, absorption speed, patent claim credibility, and INCI list transparency. Quantitative Results: The composite mean score was 3.64 with a Top-2-Box satisfaction rate of 60.1%. Notably, 94.5% of respondents agreed that the lightweight texture is suitable for Hong Kong's high-humidity environment (mean 4.14). However, trust in the proprietary 'Radiansome 100' patent technology remained moderate (mean 3.36), with 97.0% citing uncertainty of patent claims as a primary barrier. Strategic Implications: While Korean Dermatology brands dominate as reference points (81.0%), consumers demand verifiable clinical data (42.0%) and ISO GMP certification (100.0%). Addressing the gap between perceived efficacy and transparent regulatory documentation is critical for enhancing Net Promoter Score (currently -5.0) and fostering long-term loyalty in the competitive Hong Kong beauty sector.
